What is Conflict?

Conflict is a disagreement between people or groups. It can happen when people have different opinions, needs, values or expectations.

Why Does Conflict Happen?

Conflict can happen because of misunderstandings, poor communication, different opinions or competition.

Stress and strong emotions can also make disagreements more difficult to manage.

Types of Conflict

  • Interpersonal conflict
  • Group conflict
  • Online conflict
  • Internal conflict

Positive Conflict

Conflict does not always have a negative outcome. When people communicate respectfully, disagreements can help them understand different perspectives.

Conflict can also encourage people to find creative solutions and improve their relationships.
