Introduction to Conflict Mediation

Conflict mediation is a process that helps people communicate, understand different perspectives and work towards a solution.

Mediation can help people solve disagreements in a calm and respectful way.

Strategies and Solutions

Good communication is an important part of solving conflicts.

Listening carefully, staying calm and finding solutions together can help resolve disagreements.

Tips and Advice

Stay calm, listen to the other person and avoid blaming others.

Focus on solving the problem instead of making the conflict worse.
