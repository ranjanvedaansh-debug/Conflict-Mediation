What is Mediation?

Mediation is a process where a neutral person helps people involved in a conflict communicate and work towards an agreement.

Step 1 - Listen

Each person gets an opportunity to explain their point of view without being interrupted.

Listening helps everyone understand the disagreement.

Step 2 - Understand

The people involved identify their feelings, needs and concerns.

This helps them understand why the conflict happened.

Finding a Solution

After both sides have been heard, they can suggest possible solutions.

The final solution should be realistic and fair to everyone involved.

A successful mediation should end with clear communication and an agreed way forward.
