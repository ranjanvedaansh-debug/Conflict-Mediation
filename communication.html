Active Listening

Give the other person your full attention and allow them to finish speaking.

Ask questions when something is unclear instead of making assumptions.

Use "I" Statements

Explain your feelings without directly blaming another person.

Explain how you feel instead of saying what the other person always does.

Stay Calm

Staying calm can prevent a disagreement from becoming more serious.

Take a short break if emotions become too strong.

Communicate Clearly

Speak clearly and respectfully about the problem. Focus on the situation rather than attacking the person.

Good communication allows both sides to understand each other's perspectives and work towards a solution.
