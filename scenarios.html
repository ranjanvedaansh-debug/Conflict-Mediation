Group Project

Two students disagree about how to complete a group project. Both believe their idea is better.

They can listen to each other and combine the strongest parts of both ideas.

Friendship Conflict

Two friends have an argument because one feels ignored by the other.

They can communicate honestly about how they feel instead of blaming each other.

Online Conflict

A disagreement begins through messages or social media because a message is misunderstood.

Taking time before responding can prevent the situation from becoming worse.

School Disagreement

Students may disagree about rules, responsibilities or decisions made during school activities.

Speaking respectfully and involving a teacher or mediator when necessary can help solve the problem.
