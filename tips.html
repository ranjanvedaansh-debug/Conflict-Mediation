Stay Calm

Take a moment to calm down before responding to a difficult situation.

This can help you think more clearly.

Listen Carefully

Give the other person an opportunity to explain their point of view.

Try to understand their perspective before responding.

Avoid Blaming

Blaming another person can make the conflict worse.

Focus on the problem and how it can be solved.

Find a Solution Together

Think about solutions that consider the needs of everyone involved.

A solution created together is more likely to be accepted by everyone.

Remember that solving a conflict may take time and patience.
