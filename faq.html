What is conflict?

Conflict is a disagreement between people or groups. It can happen because of different opinions, needs, values or expectations.

What is mediation?

Mediation is a process where a neutral person helps people communicate and work towards a solution.

Why is communication important?

Communication helps people understand different perspectives and express their feelings and needs.

How can I resolve a conflict?

Stay calm, listen carefully and explain your point of view respectfully.

Try to find a solution that considers the needs of everyone involved.

If the conflict cannot be solved alone, ask a trusted adult, teacher or mediator for help.
